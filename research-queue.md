- Online records request form (linked from that page)

**Ask them to search:**

- Decedent: **Fern A. DeVeny** / **Fern A. Deveny** / **Fern A. Farmer DeVeny**
- Date of death: **18 Jul 1986**
- County: Multnomah
- Return: probate, estate, or small-estate **case number and complete file**

The newly archived Fern funeral-notice capture should be transcribed first. Still pull Fern’s **death certificate** for residence/place-of-death confirmation because an obituary/funeral notice does not necessarily state or prove where death occurred. This is the strongest documentary path to C10.
