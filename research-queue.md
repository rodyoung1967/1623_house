## 1. Fern A. DeVeny probate / small estate (1986)

**Why:** Could show residence, heirs, how the house passed after 18 Jul 1986.

**Where:** [Multnomah Circuit Court Records](https://www.courts.oregon.gov/courts/multnomah/records/pages/default.aspx)

- Email: MUL.Records@ojd.state.or.us
- Phone: 971-274-0570 (Mon–Fri 8:30–10:30am or 1:30–4:00pm)
- Walk-in: 1200 SW 1st Ave, Room 3315, 8:00am–4:00pm
- Online records request form (linked from that page)

**Ask them to search:**

- Decedent: **Fern A. DeVeny** / **Fern A. Deveny** / **Fern A. Farmer DeVeny**
- Date of death: **18 Jul 1986**
- County: Multnomah
- Return: probate, estate, or small-estate **case number and complete file**

Inventory basis: closed probate including small estates **1985–2011** exists; circuit-court indexes cover probate from **1984**. No public web case number found. That is not evidence of no file. ([W16](https://sos.oregon.gov/archives/county-records-guide/Pages/multnomah-inventory.aspx), excerpt in `sources/ingested/research-notes/multnomah-probate-inventory-excerpt.md`)

Also pull Fern’s **obituary** and **death certificate** (Oregon Center for Health Statistics) for residence and place of death — resolves C10.

## 2. Recorder: 1986–1988 title into Nola

**Why:** How Nola acquired the house after Fern’s death.

**Where:** [Recording & Customer Service](https://multco.us/departments/recording-customer-service) · 503-988-2273 · clerk@multco.us · 501 SE Hawthorne Blvd Suite 175.

Electronic **index** from 1965. Official DART rule ([W22](https://multco.us/info/property-records-and-recording)): deeds **dated after 12/31/2001** can be printed from home. Images of older records: order from DART or use lobby computers. **1992 and 1999 deeds are both pre-cutoff.**

**Search:** 18 Jul 1986 through ~1988 · parcel **R193918** · grantor Fern A. DeVeny / estate of Fern DeVeny · grantee Nola DeVeny or Nola D. Dowty. Retrieve every instrument, not just a deed.
