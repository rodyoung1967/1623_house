## Construction and first owner

The City of Portland plumbing card records a new one-story frame dwelling on Lots 27 & 28, Block 33, Jonesmore. First inspection **2 Mar 1912**, final **13 Mar 1913**.

- **Sources:** P1, W1
- **Status:** accepted; C01 resolved

### 1912 — “Mr. J. Deveny” named as owner

The owner line on P1 reads **Mr. J. Deveny**. The leading M is the title “Mr.”, not a separate given-name initial.

- **Sources:** P1
- **Status:** transcription resolved (C02); identifying him as James Benjamin DeVeny remains **C03**

### Pre-1933 — Earlier street number “369”

P1 visibly shows **369**, later annotated with **1623 N.E.** S2022 gives the full old form as **369 E 73rd Ave N**, but that exact string still needs the official directory/renumbering record.

### 21 Sep 1920 — James Goodwin notice identifies Sophia DeVeny

*Oregon Journal* death notice for **James Goodwin** says he died 18 Sep 1920 and identifies his daughter as **Mrs. Sophia DeVeny of 102 Dakota Street**.

- **Sources:** W34
- **Status:** accepted family/address evidence; resolves C16 in favor of **Goodwin**

## Heat, deaths, and later occupancy

### 1962 onward — House visible on aerials

Portland Maps aerials show the house from 1962 through 2025; a rear structure is visible at least by 1996.

- **Sources:** M1962–M2025
- **Status:** accepted site presence, not title

### 1966 — Oil heat; 275-gallon basement tank

Fire Prevention Division oil-burner permit at 1623: warm-air heat; **275-gallon basement tank**; installed for **E. A. DeVeny**.

- **Sources:** P6
- **Status:** permit facts accepted; Fern title inference C07 and month C08 remain open

### May / June 1967 — Denise Genevieve Dowty; Nola household in Milwaukie

Two contemporary newspaper notices agree on identity and family but disagree on Denise's birth date:

- W29, *Oregon Journal*, 7 Jun 1967: **23 May 1967**, Bess Kaiser Hospital; Mr. and Mrs. Lloyd Wallace Dowty **of Milwaukie**; siblings Bruce and Diane; DeVeny/Dowty grandparents.
- W38, *Oregonian*, 8 Jun 1967: **25 May 1967**; Denise Genevieve; siblings Bruce and Diane; same grandparents.

- **Sources:** W29, W38, W27
- **Status:** Milwaukie residence/family accepted; birth date **disputed (C18)**
- **House significance:** Nola had a separate Milwaukie household by 1967

### 24–28 Apr 1969 — Edgar dies; residence given as 1623

Edgar A. DeVeny died **24 Apr 1969**. W27 (*Oregon Journal*, 26 Apr) and W28 (28 Apr) give **1623 NE 73rd Ave** as his residence, name Fern A. as wife, and Irene F. Taylor / Nola D. Dowty as daughters.

- **Sources:** W27, W28, W2, W3, W13
- **Status:** accepted; strong address anchor

### 1969–1986 — Elderly woman remembered at 1623 is very likely Fern

Neighbor O2 said they knew Fern and identified the older woman living alone at the house as Fern. Documentary context strongly supports that identification: Edgar died at 1623 in 1969, while Nola is documented in Milwaukie in 1967 and again in 1987.

- **Sources:** O2, O3, W27, W29, W40
- **Status:** working conclusion **resolved as C19: Fern, not Nola**
- **Caution:** exact date Fern stopped residing at 1623 remains unknown

### 18 / 22 Jul 1986 — Fern dies at Friendship Care Center

W39 (*Oregonian*, 22 Jul 1986) says **Fern A. DeVeny died 18 Jul 1986 at Friendship Care Center**, age 80. It names daughters **Irene Fern Taylor of Portland** and **Nola Diane Dowty of Milwaukie**. The obituary also mentions years of square dancing and gardening.

- **Sources:** W39, W4, W14, O2
- **Status:** place of death documented; C10 resolved
- **Oral-history correction:** O2's “died in the house” detail is contradicted by W39; Fern identification remains accepted under C19
- **Open:** last residence / date of entering care; birthplace conflict **Cambridge vs Midvale, Idaho (C20)**

### 17 Sep 1987 — Irene Taylor obituary again places Nola in Milwaukie

W40 obituary for **Irene Fern DeVeny Taylor**, age 65, identifies sister **Nola Dowty of Milwaukie**. It also records Irene's Grant High/UO background, WWII shipyard welding, Oregon Journal classified-ad work, and Ikebana activity.

- **Sources:** W40
- **Status:** accepted family and residence-elsewhere evidence
- **House significance:** Nola is independently documented away from 1623 one year after Fern's death
