Nola is not named in the notice. Combined with Edgar’s 1969 death notice naming **Nola D. Dowty** as his daughter (W27), Mrs. Lloyd Wallace Dowty is **Nola** at 4/5–5/5.

This shows that by June 1967 the Dowty household was in **Milwaukie**. It supports the neighbor’s later recollection (O2) that Nola had her own household and was not simply remaining at 1623 with Edgar and Fern. It does **not** prove she never returned to 1623 after Fern’s death in 1986 (C11).
