## Portland city directories (MCL)

https://multcolib.org/who-lived-my-house-find-past-residents-portland-city-directories

- **1930–present:** reverse “Street and Avenue Guide” (often pink or green paper). Street → block → address.
- **1930–1931:** use the **pre-renumbering** address (working lead: **369** / 369 E 73rd Ave N — C04).
- **1932–1933:** check **both** old and new addresses; neighborhoods converted at different times. Citywide finish July 1933 (W10).
- **1929 and earlier:** no reverse section. Search by name (work backward from 1930 residents) or use plumbing/sewer owner names (P1).
- Listings are usually heads of household; wives often first name only in parentheses.

Priority years for 1623: 1912–1913 (C03), 1939–1947 (C06), 1966–1986 (Fern), 1986–1992 (tenants).

## Property records / DART

https://multco.us/info/property-records-and-recording

- Deeds **dated after 12/31/2001** can be printed from home.
- **1992 and 1999 deeds are older than that cutoff.** Order from DART Customer Service or view/print on lobby computers.
- 1999 sale still cannot be a home download even though it is “recent” relative to 1912.

Inventory of what collections exist: https://sos.oregon.gov/archives/county-records-guide/Pages/multnomah-inventory.aspx
