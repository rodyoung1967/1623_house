## What this proves for 1623

- Edgar, Fern, and Nola **lived at 1623 on 1 Apr 1950**. Occupancy, not a deed.
- **Irene** (age ~27) is **not** in this household. She had left since the 1940 census (P7).
- Nola was still an **unmarried daughter at home** eight months before the 31 Dec 1950 Fick wedding (W26).
- Edgar’s work as a **printer** matches the 1957 Polk listing (Ivy Press, W24).
- Nola **age 16** on 1 Apr 1950 → born about **Apr 1933–Apr 1934**. Ancestry’s “1934” is computed from that age, not a birth record. Additional evidence for C15; does not close it.
