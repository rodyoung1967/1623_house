# House history: 1623 NE 73rd Ave

A sourced narrative of **1623 NE 73rd Ave, Portland, OR 97213**. This is not a title report. Every claim has a source ID. IDs are defined in [sources.md](sources.md). Working readings and open conflicts are in [decisions.md](decisions.md) and [CONFLICTS.md](CONFLICTS.md).

Oral history is labeled **oral**. Census and city directories are **occupancy**, not proof of legal title ([decisions.md](decisions.md) C05).

The house stands on **Lots 27 and 28, Block 33, Jonesmore**, between Halsey and Broadway (P1, P4). The current Multnomah tax account is **R193918** (S2026b; confirm on a county printout). Public-record aggregators list it as built 1912, about **1,102 sq ft**, 2 bed, 1 bath, on a **5,724 sq ft** lot (W1, W15).

The builder is unknown. In 1907 George D. Schalk advertised Jonesmore lots and offered to build for buyers (W6). That is neighborhood marketing, not a builder attribution for this house. In April 1912 the *Sunday Oregonian* reported a building boom in North Jonesmore (W7). That article does not name this lot.

## 1912: a new one-story frame dwelling

A City of Portland plumbing card records a **new one-story frame dwelling** on this legal description (P1). First inspection **2 Mar 1912**, final **13 Mar 1913** ([decisions.md](decisions.md) C01). The card lists dwelling use and handwritten fixture and drain notes (P1).

The owner line on the same card is **Mr. J. Deveny** ([decisions.md](decisions.md) C02). Identifying that man as James Benjamin DeVeny (1871–1945) is a family lead, not proven (C03; S2022, W12). Sophia Charlotte DeVeny is not named on any permit in this archive (S2022).

The card also shows an earlier street number **369**, later overwritten or annotated as **1623 N.E.** (P1). Portland finished citywide renumbering in July 1933 (W10). The 2022 notes give the full old form as **369 E 73rd Ave N** (S2022). The number 369 is on the card; the exact old street string is still open (C04).

## 1939–1947: sewer and water

Before 1939 the house used a cesspool (P2). On **26 Sep 1939** it was changed to city sewer, sewer permit **41973** (P2, P4, P5). The building was then described as a one-story old frame residence (P2). The Public Works record matches this address and Jonesmore Lots 27–28, Block 33; the applicant was **Geo. L. Melvine** (P4). The owner line on the 1939 plumbing card is spelled **“Mr. De Verney”** (P2).

On **7 Feb 1947** a water-service replacement was inspected (permit **185319**); final **14 Feb 1947** (P3). The owner on that card is **“Mrs. Deveny.”** The work is certain. Whether that woman is Fern is still open (C06).

A 1 Nov 1945 funeral notice for **James B. DeVeny** gives his home as **0214 SW Dakota Street**, not 1623 (W33). He was living elsewhere by 1945. That does not settle whether he was the 1912 “Mr. J. Deveny” (C03).

## The daughters leave; Edgar and Fern stay

On **31 Dec 1950** Nola Diane DeVeny married **Jack Howard Fick**, son of the George S. Ficks (*Oregon Journal*, 12 Jan 1951, p. 15) (W26). The notice names her parents as **Mr. and Mrs. E. A. DeVeny** and says the couple is “now at home here,” meaning the Portland paper’s market, not proof she stayed at 1623 (W26).

She later married **Lloyd Wallace Dowty**, son of Mr. and Mrs. Willard D. Dowty, at Grant Park Baptist Church (W30). The compiled 2026-08-17 Word history dates that paper 26 Sept 1952 and infers the wedding **20 Sept 1952** (S2026c). How the Fick marriage ended is not documented.

Their children, with Edgar and Fern named as maternal grandparents:

| Child | Born | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bruce Edgar (1957 notice: Bruce Lloyd) | 2 Oct 1954, Wilcox Memorial | W31, W32 |
| Diane Fern | 8 Feb 1957, Wilcox Memorial | W32 |
| Denise Genevieve | 23 May 1967, Bess Kaiser; parents **of Milwaukie** | W29 |

By **June 1967** the Dowty household was in **Milwaukie**, not at 1623 (W29). Combined with Edgar’s 1969 death notice naming **Nola D. Dowty** as his daughter (W27), Mrs. Lloyd Wallace Dowty is Nola. That does not prove she never returned to 1623 after 1986 (C11).

Polk city directories still list **Edgar A. (Fern A.)** at **1623 NE 73d** in 1956, 1957, and 1959 (W23, W24, W25). In 1957 Edgar is a **printer at Ivy Press** (W24). The 1959 reverse list puts **DeVeny Edgar A.** at 1623, phone **AL 2-4971**, between 1616 and 1628, before Broadway (W25). Irene and Nola do not appear as heads of household there. These listings are occupancy from OCR, not title. (S2026c does not use these directories.)

## 1960s: oil heat, and the house on aerials

Portland Maps aerials show the house on the west side of NE 73rd from **1962** through **2025**, with **1611** immediately south (M1962–M2025). A smaller rear structure is visible at least by 1996 (M1996). A Portland Archives neighborhood aerial (A2010-001.86) was captioned in 2022 as the first known photo of the house; it is a wide labeled view, not a close portrait of this dwelling (M1910s, S2022).

In **1966** a Fire Prevention Division oil-burner permit at this address records **warm-air oil heat** and a **275-gallon basement tank**, installed for **E. A. DeVeny** (P6). The permit facts are certain; that E.A. is Edgar is 4/5 (P6). Whether Fern was an owner, and whether the month is July or September, remain open (C07, C08).

## 1969: Edgar dies; the paper gives this address

**Edgar A. DeVeny** died **24 Apr 1969** (W3, W13). The *Oregon Journal* death notice of **26 Apr 1969** reads (W27):

> DeVENY — Edgar A., 1623 NE 73rd Ave.; husband of Fern A.; father of Irene F. Taylor and Nola D. Dowty; 2 brothers and 2 sisters; 4 grandchildren. Services Tuesday, 2:30 pm, CALDWELL’S COLONIAL MORTUARY, Sandy blvd at 14th. Friends invited. Vault interment Willamette National cemetery.

A shorter funeral notice on **28 Apr 1969** repeats the address and service (W28; W2 is the newspapers.com index of that notice). The address is printed as **Edgar’s residence**. Irene F. Taylor and Nola D. Dowty are named as daughters, not as occupants of 1623 (W27). His stone at Willamette National Cemetery reads CPL CO E 63 INF 11 DIV; 16 Sep 1895 – 24 Apr 1969 (W13).

## 1969–1986: Fern

**Fern A. Farmer DeVeny** was born 29 Jan 1906 and died **18 Jul 1986** (W4, W14). A next-door neighbor who knew Fern said she **died in the house in 1986**, and that the “old crazy lady” mentioned by Randy and Christine **was Fern** (O2, O3, S2022). That is strong oral history. Place of death is not yet on a death certificate or obituary in this archive (C10).

How title moved after Fern’s death — probate, or a deed to Nola — has not been retrieved (S2026b; [research-queue.md](research-queue.md)).

## 1992–1999: Irvin purchase, then Young

Public records report a sale on **1 Dec 1992** for **$64,950** (W1). That date and price match the 2022 notes (S2022). Working record until the deed is in hand: **Nola D. Dowty** sold to **Wayne Irvin et al.** (S2022, W1). Wayne is **Christine M. Irvin’s father** (O1). Christine was likely among the “et al.” grantees; the recorded instruments must confirm that ([decisions.md](decisions.md) C12). Randy is named with Christine as previous owners in the 2022 notes (O3); his legal role is open (C13).

The current owner says the house was tenant-occupied at that purchase and that Christine did **not** rent it afterward (O1). Tenant names are unknown (O1).

Sometime **1992–1999**, an unnamed informant said the oil tank was removed and **given to the owners of 1611 NE 73rd**, likely when a gas furnace was installed (O4). **1611 is the south neighbor**, not this house (M1962). That tank was the **275-gallon basement tank** documented in 1966 (P6). There was **no underground storage tank** at 1623 ([decisions.md](decisions.md) C09).

Public records report a sale on **27 May 1999** for **$127,000** (W1). The 2022 notes give **1 May 1999**, **Rodney A. Young ET AL**, from **Christine M. Irvin**, same price (S2022). The owner recalls summer or July 1999 (O1). Price is accepted; the exact day and who “ET AL” is remain open (C14).
