### James Benjamin DeVeny (1871–1945)

- **Genealogy lead:** spouse Sophia; family-tree material links him to Edgar Arthur DeVeny (S2022, W12).
- **1945:** W33 gives his home as **0214 SW Dakota Street**, not 1623.
- **2 Nov 1945 *Oregonian* obituary (W35):** full name **James Benjamin DeVeny**; born **14 Aug 1871** in **Memphis, Tennessee**; came to Portland about 61 years earlier; he and Mrs. DeVeny had celebrated their **53rd wedding anniversary** the prior year; sons included **E. A., L. R., and Howard DeVeny**; nine grandchildren.
- **House claim:** S2022 says the house appears built for James and Sophia. The new family evidence strengthens the lineage but still does not place James Benjamin at the Jonesmore parcel in 1912 (C03).
- **Sources:** S2022, W12, W33, W35
- **Status:** likely family connection; disputed as the specific 1912 owner

### Sophia Charlotte Goodwin DeVeny (1872–1952)

## Edgar and Fern household at 1623

### Edgar Arthur DeVeny (16 Sep 1895 – 24 Apr 1969)

- **1940 original census:** head of household at **1623**, age 44 (P7). Original schedule is **ED 37-308, sheet 1A, line 16**, enumerated 2 Apr 1940. The household row reports the home **owned**, value **$1,500**; this is census-reported tenure, not deed title.
- **1950:** head at 1623; age 54; printer at a private printing company (P8).
- **1951:** named with Fern as Nola's parents, “Mr. and Mrs. E. A. DeVeny” (W26).
- **1956–1959:** Polk directories list Edgar A. (Fern A.) at 1623 NE 73d; 1957 occupation **printer at Ivy Press**; 1959 phone **AL 2-4971** (W23–W25).
- **1966:** oil-burner permit installed for **E. A. DeVeny** (P6) — 4/5 this is Edgar.
- **1969:** death/funeral notices give residence **1623 NE 73rd Ave**; husband of Fern A.; father of Irene F. Taylor and Nola D. Dowty (W27, W28).
- **Military stone:** CPL CO E 63 INF 11 DIV; 16 Sep 1895 – 24 Apr 1969 (W13).
- **Father/family:** W35 independently names an **E. A. DeVeny** among James Benjamin DeVeny's surviving sons, consistent with the genealogy chain.
- **Sources:** P6, P7, P8, W2, W3, W13, W23–W28, W35, S2022

### Fern A. Farmer DeVeny (29 Jan 1906 – 18 Jul 1986)

- **1940:** wife, age 34, at 1623 (P7).
- **1950:** wife, age 44, at 1623; indexed **Fenn A.** (P8).
- **1947:** P3 names owner only as **“Mrs. Deveny.”** Fern is a strong candidate but not explicitly identified (C06).
- **1956–1959:** listed with Edgar at 1623 in Polk directories (W23–W25).
- **1969:** named as Edgar's surviving wife in the death notice that gives his residence as 1623 (W27).
- **Elderly resident identification:** the neighbor who knew Fern identified the older woman living alone at the house as **Fern** (O2). Combined with Nola's separate Milwaukie household, this is the accepted working conclusion: **very likely Fern, not Nola** (C19 resolved).
- **Place of death:** W39, *Oregonian*, 22 Jul 1986, says Fern **died 18 Jul at Friendship Care Center**, age 80. This corrects O2's “died in the house” recollection (C10 resolved).
- **W39 biography:** resident of Portland since youth; active in **square-dance clubs** for many years; enjoyed **gardening**; married 48 years to Edgar; daughters **Irene Fern Taylor of Portland** and **Nola Diane Dowty of Milwaukie**; four grandchildren; burial Willamette National Cemetery.
- **Birthplace conflict:** W39 says **Cambridge, Idaho**; W14/Find a Grave says **Midvale, Washington County, Idaho** (C20).
- **Still needed:** last verified date of residence at 1623 / date of entering care; death certificate and probate.
- **Sources:** P7, P8, W4, W14, W23–W25, W27, W39, O2

## Daughters of Edgar and Fern

- **1940:** Irene DeVeny, daughter, age 17, at 1623 (P7).
- **1950:** not in the 1623 household (P8).

- **1987 obituary (W40):** **Irene Fern DeVeny Taylor**, age 65; native of Portland; Grant High graduate; attended University of Oregon; worked as a **shipyard welder during World War II**; later worked in the old **Oregon Journal classified-ad department**; traveled in mainland China in 1982; active in **Ikebana** Japanese flower arranging.

### Nola Diane DeVeny / Fick / Dowty (d. 9 Oct 2000)

One person. Child/young-adult occupant of 1623; later working 1992 grantor.

- **1940:** daughter, age 6, at 1623 (P7).
- **1950:** unmarried daughter, age 16, at 1623 (P8).
- **Birth year:** cemetery/S2022 favor **1933**; census ages imply ~1933–34; W11 says 1930 — still C15.
- **Preferred given name:** **Nola Diane** (W26, W37, W5, W27). `Diana Nola` is retained only as an alternate from a family-tree screenshot.
- **First marriage:** **31 Dec 1950** to **Jack Howard Fick** (W26). How the marriage ended remains undocumented.
- **4 Nov 1952:** *Oregonian* marriage-license listing explicitly gives **Nola D. DeVeny, 1623 N.E. 73d** (W36). This is the latest direct address-specific record currently placing Nola at the parental home.
- **Second marriage:** **8 Nov 1952** to **Lloyd Wallace Dowty** at Grant Park Baptist Church (W37). This corrects the older September inference from W30/S2026c.
- **Children:** Bruce Edgar/Bruce Lloyd (W31/W32); Diane Fern (W32); Denise Genevieve (W29/W38, exact birth day disputed C18).
- **1967:** W29 identifies Mr. and Mrs. Lloyd Wallace Dowty as **of Milwaukie**.
- **1969:** Edgar's death notice names **Nola D. Dowty** as daughter (W27), not as an occupant of 1623.
- **1987:** Irene's obituary identifies **Nola Dowty of Milwaukie** (W40).
- **Neighbor O2:** said Nola never moved back with Lloyd/Diane. Documentary evidence strongly supports a separate household but does not prove every intervening year (C11).
- **1992 grantor — working record:** Nola D. Dowty sold 1 Dec 1992 for $64,950 to Wayne Irvin et al.; deed not yet in archive (C12).
- **Sources:** P7, P8, W1, W5, W11, W26–W32, W36–W38, W40, O1, O2, S2022, S2026, S2026b, S2026c
