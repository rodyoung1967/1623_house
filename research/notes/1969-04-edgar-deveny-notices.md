# Edgar A. DeVeny death / funeral notices, April 1969

**Source IDs:** W27 (26 Apr), W28 (28 Apr)  
**Paper:** *Oregon Journal* (filenames). The 28 Apr page also shows an *Oregonian* classifieds header; W2 already indexed the 28 Apr *Oregon Daily Journal* item.

## W27 — 26 Apr 1969 (fuller death notice)

> DeVENY — Edgar A., 1623 NE 73rd Ave.; husband of Fern A.; father of Irene F. Taylor and Nola D. Dowty; 2 brothers and 2 sisters; 4 grandchildren. Services Tuesday, 2:30 pm, CALDWELL’S COLONIAL MORTUARY, Sandy blvd at 14th. Friends invited. Vault interment Willamette National cemetery.

26 Apr 1969 was a Saturday; “Services Tuesday” = **29 Apr 1969**.

## W28 — 28 Apr 1969 (shorter funeral notice)

> DeVENY — Edgar A., 1623 NE 73rd Ave. Services Tuesday, 2:30 pm, CALDWELL’S COLONIAL MORTUARY, Sandy Blvd. at 14th. Friends invited. Vault interment Willamette National Cemetery.

This is the clipping behind the W2 newspapers.com index (same date, same address).

## What 1623 means here

The address is printed as **Edgar’s residence** on both notices. That upgrades the address association from an index line (W2) to the actual text.

The notices do **not** state Edgar's place of death. They also do **not** say Irene or Nola lived at 1623. Irene and Nola are listed as his daughters. Fern is named as wife; that is not a separate address line for her, and it is not a title record.
