## W34 — Oregon Journal, 21 Sep 1920 — James Goodwin death notice

**Image:** `photos/genealogy/1920-09-21-oregon-journal-deveney.png`

Visible notice:

- **James Goodwin** died in Portland **18 Sep 1920**, age **78**.
- He is identified as the father of **Mrs. Sophia DeVeny of 102 Dakota Street** and **Robert Goodwin of Pullman, Washington**.
- Funeral announced for 22 Sep at Holman’s chapel; interment Rose City Cemetery.

**Importance:** This is contemporary newspaper evidence that Sophia DeVeny was a daughter of **James Goodwin**, strongly resolving the Goodwin/Godwin spelling conflict in favor of **Goodwin** (C16). It does **not** itself prove that Sophia or James Benjamin DeVeny owned 1623 in 1912.

## W35 — Oregonian, 2 Nov 1945 — James Benjamin DeVeny obituary

**Image:** `photos/genealogy/1945-james-b-deveny-funeral-notice-oregonian.png`

Visible facts:

- **James Benjamin DeVeny** died Wednesday at his home, **0214 SW Dakota Street**.
- Born **14 Aug 1871** in **Memphis, Tennessee**.
- Came to Portland about **61 years** before his death.
- He and Mrs. DeVeny had celebrated their **53rd wedding anniversary** the previous year.
- Member of Trinity Presbyterian Church and Samaritan Lodge, I.O.O.F.
- Survived by his widow, two daughters, three sons including **E. A., L. R., and Howard DeVeny**, and nine grandchildren.
- Funeral at Finley’s chapel; vault entombment Riverview Abbey mausoleum.

**Importance:** Corroborates W33’s Dakota Street residence and independently links an **E. A. DeVeny** son to James Benjamin. It strengthens the family reconstruction but does not by itself identify the 1912 `Mr. J. Deveny` on P1.

## W39 — Oregonian, 22 Jul 1986 — Fern A. DeVeny obituary

**Image:** `photos/genealogy/fern-deveny-funeral-notice-oregonian.png`

Visible facts:

- **Fern A. DeVeny** died **18 Jul 1986** at **Friendship Care Center**.
- Age **80**.
- Obituary says she was born in **Cambridge, Idaho**.
- Resident of Portland since her youth.
- Active in square-dance clubs for many years and enjoyed gardening.
- Married **48 years** to **Edgar A. DeVeny**, who died in 1969.
- Daughters: **Irene Fern Taylor of Portland** and **Nola Diane Dowty of Milwaukie**.
- Four grandchildren.
- Burial at Willamette National Cemetery.

**Importance:** This resolves the place-of-death issue: Fern **did not die at 1623**; the obituary places her death at Friendship Care Center. The neighbor’s recollection that she “died in the house” is therefore inaccurate on that detail. The same oral history remains strong for identifying the long-time elderly woman as **Fern**, not Nola (C19). Fern’s **last date of residence at 1623 remains unknown**.

**Additional conflict:** W39 says **Cambridge, Idaho** while W14/Find a Grave says **Midvale, Washington County, Idaho**. Keep birthplace unresolved pending a birth record or stronger source (C20).

## W40 — Oregonian, 17 Sep 1987 — Irene F. D. Taylor obituary

**Image:** `photos/genealogy/irene-f-taylor-funeral-notice.png`

Visible facts:

- **Irene Fern DeVeny Taylor** died in 1987 at age **65**.
- Native of Portland; graduate of Grant High School; attended the University of Oregon.
- During World War II worked in the shipyards as a **welder**.
- After the war worked in the classified-ad department of the old **Oregon Journal**, leaving to raise a family.
- Traveled in mainland China in 1982 and was active in **Ikebana** Japanese flower arranging.
- Survivors include husband **Roy** of Portland, son **Jeff** of Portland, and sister **Nola Dowty of Milwaukie**.
- Interment at Finley’s Sunset Hills Cemetery.

**Importance to 1623:** The obituary independently places **Nola Dowty in Milwaukie in September 1987**, one year after Fern’s death. Together with W29 (Milwaukie in 1967), this strongly supports that Nola was not the elderly woman living alone at 1623 and was not living there immediately after Fern’s death.

## Findings promoted from this review

1. **P7 upgraded:** original 1940 schedule, ED 37-308, sheet 1A, lines 16–19; household reported **owning** the $1,500 home.
2. **C16 resolved:** Sophia’s family surname is **Goodwin**, supported by her father James Goodwin’s 1920 death notice.
3. **Nola documented at 1623 on 4 Nov 1952** (W36).
4. **Nola married Lloyd Dowty on 8 Nov 1952** (W37), correcting the earlier September inference.
5. **Nola/Lloyd were in Milwaukie by 1967**, and Nola is again explicitly **of Milwaukie in 1987** (W29, W40).
6. **Fern died at Friendship Care Center on 18 Jul 1986**, not in the house (W39).
7. The elderly long-time woman remembered at 1623 is nevertheless **very likely Fern, not Nola** (C19), because the neighbor knew Fern and the documentary record places Nola elsewhere.
8. Denise Genevieve Dowty has a **23 May vs 25 May 1967 newspaper conflict** (C18).
9. Fern’s birthplace has a **Cambridge vs Midvale, Idaho conflict** (C20).
